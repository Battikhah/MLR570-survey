\section{All Included Methods}
\label{app:methods}

This appendix lists all 112 methods that the systematic search included (\S\ref{sec:intro}), with the best
result each paper reports on the standard pairs. The main tables in \S\ref{sec:benchmarks} keep only the
top rows and the comparison-set baselines; the rest are here. Rows follow the full leaderboard order:
CIFAR-100 ResNet32$\times$4$\to$ResNet8$\times$4, then ImageNet-only methods, then data-free methods, then
methods with no tabulated pair.
