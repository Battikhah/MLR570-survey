\section{Open Challenges and Future Directions}
\label{sec:future}

\paragraph{A comparable leaderboard.}
The tables are not a controlled comparison (Section~\ref{sec:bench-protocol}). On ResNet32$\times$4$\to$ResNet8$\times$4,
classical KD is 73.33\% in DKD's table \citep{zhao2022dkd}, and 74.75\% in SKD's \citep{jeong2026skd}, a spread wider than the gaps between most single logit methods in
Table~\ref{tab:bench-cifar}. Cross-paper reproductions also differ by 0.3--0.5 points (Section~\ref{sec:prelim-benchmarks}); this is distinct from variation across training seeds. Stacking adds a second problem. Three of the five highest CIFAR-100 rows are stacked
(DeepKD on CRLD, EA-MLD with label smoothing, MLKD with logit standardisation;
\citealp{huang2025deepkd,su2025eakd,sun2024lskd}), and several plug-ins lose much of their gain alone: EA-KD gives
75.46\% against 78.38\% stacked. We found no
included paper with a full grid of plug-ins against bases. \emph{Direction:} re-run the methods
with public code in one code base, with fixed teachers and five seeds; check by rank correlation whether
the published order survives, and train every pairing of a few bases (KD, DKD, MLKD) and plug-ins (logit standardisation, DOT, a reliability
weight).

\paragraph{Which teacher signal to trust.}
The reliability methods disagree about what reliability means: label agreement, label probability, entropy or validation-gradient alignment
\citep{meng2019conditionalts,mishra2021cckd,su2025eakd,sumit2026chad}. Reported examples include RLD at 76.64\% and MCD at 76.81\%; KD-AIF uses an updated teacher and is not directly comparable (Section~\ref{sec:bench-families}). We found no paper that tests whether
these signals pick the same samples. \emph{Direction:} on one fixed teacher, compare sample-weight rankings under a preregistered top-$k$ or threshold rule, then test whether removing the selected examples changes student accuracy.

\paragraph{How to combine the objectives, and whether they conflict.}
KD papers do not compare the label-priority, distillation-priority and symmetric rules under one protocol (\S\ref{sec:kd_moo_critical}). Only DOT compares with a sweep of fixed task weights \citep{zhao2023dot}; three multi-task studies found tuned scalarisation hard to beat
\citep{xin2022mtohelp,kurin2022unitary,royer2023scalarization}. PCD \citep{varam2026pcd} has no reported
distillation result; whether it improves distillation remains untested. The only direct measurement of conflict we found is DHO's, with a zero-shot CLIP teacher and 16
labelled examples per class, where the final-epoch cosine at the classifier is $-0.76$ \citep{kang2025dho}; for
the full-label protocol with a trained CNN teacher we found none. Scale may explain some gains (PCD's pruning results, TPKD's 0.22-point projection ablation; \citealp{varam2026pcd,yuan2026tpkd}).
\emph{Direction:} run one rule from each group on KD and a strong logit loss against a tuned weighted sum,
logging the CE--KD cosine and norm ratio, and compare each rule with its implied rescaling alone.

\paragraph{Data-free distillation and the ImageNet gap.}
Nine of the ten included data-free records have WRN-40-2$\to$WRN-16-2 values; HiDFD reports a different pair (Section~\ref{sec:bench-datafree}). Synthesis budgets and data sources differ across these records. They do not provide a matched comparison with the main ResNet32$\times$4$\to$ResNet8$\times$4 table. On ImageNet, most ResNet34$\to$ResNet18 rows fall between 71.0\% and 73.0\%,
close to DKD's 71.70\% \citep{zhao2023dot}, so CIFAR-100 gains of two or more points mostly shrink to under one.
\emph{Direction:} report data-free and data-based methods on the same pairs and teacher, and report absolute gains over matched KD baselines on each dataset.

\paragraph{Metrics and settings beyond top-1 accuracy.}
The benchmark tables report top-1 accuracy, and the KD temperature is distinct from calibration (Section~\ref{sec:prelim-temperature}, \citealp{guo2017calibration}), yet the only expected calibration error quoted in
Section~\ref{sec:logit} is LumiNet's, 0.06 against 0.11 for KD \citep{hossain2025luminet}. Reliability methods are judged by accuracy, not by whether teacher errors transfer. Training cost is rarely reported, though DOT needs two backward passes per step and searched
distillers a search per pair \citep{zhao2023dot}.
\label{sec:emerging}Other settings remain outside scope; DHO reports lower CE--KD conflict with separate heads for a zero-shot CLIP teacher \citep{kang2025dho}.
\emph{Direction:} in the re-run, also report calibration error, corrupted-test accuracy, agreement with
the teacher's errors and time per epoch.
