\section{Teacher Reliability and Selective Transfer}
\label{sec:reliability}

Teacher reliability is not one property: agreement with a label, probability on that label, calibration, and usefulness to a given student measure different things. Conditional Teacher-Student Learning (CTSL) uses teacher targets only when the teacher predicts the observed label, while Confidence Conditioned KD (CCKD, an arXiv preprint) uses the teacher's probability on that label as a sample weight or to blend teacher and one-hot targets, and its self-regulation variant can skip examples the student already handles \citep{meng2019conditionalts,mishra2021cckd}. CTSL makes a discrete correctness decision; CCKD keeps graded confidence. Both rely on observed labels, so label errors corrupt the signal, and correctness says nothing about calibration, student compatibility, or the value of the other class probabilities. % INTERPRETATION: limits of a top-class correctness gate.

Hard Gate KD routes language-generation supervision by comparing the student's probability on the true token or sentence with the calibrated teacher's, switching between teacher and label \citep{lee2022hardgatekd}. Unlike CTSL it uses the student's state and calibration; unlike CCKD it is a hard decision, and the label branch may be inactive when the teacher is selected, so no per-example ground-truth loss is guaranteed. Calibration does not ensure reliability under distribution shift, and the post-hoc calibration temperature plays a different role from the KD temperature \citep{guo2017calibration,ovadia2019shiftuncertainty}. With several teachers, CA-MKD weights each teacher per sample by its cross-entropy against the label and transfers logits and features \citep{zhang2022camkd}; we could not confirm from the paper whether a student cross-entropy term is kept on every example. % VERIFIED: inspected paper does not settle this loss detail.

Newer methods widen the signal. TGeo-KD sets a per-sample teacher/label fusion ratio from the geometry of student, teacher and label predictions, a soft mixture rather than CTSL's wholesale replacement, though the ratio is still a proxy \citep{hu2024tgeokd}. EA-KD weights examples by teacher and student entropy, up-weighting high-entropy samples without a threshold; entropy does not say which class relations to trust \citep{su2025eakd}. LTKD works at the group level, rebalancing cross-group information so a head-biased teacher does not suppress tail classes \citep{kim2026ltkd}, and AGRE-KD selects among several teachers using gradients against a biased model to target worst-group accuracy \citep{kenfack2025agrekd}; both belong to biased-group settings. TPKD (a 2026 preprint) projects teacher directions to preserve task margins, asking whether an update threatens the task rather than whether the teacher looks uncertain, which links it to the update rules of \S\ref{sec:kd_moo} \citep{yuan2026tpkd}.

Other methods repair targets or estimate student-specific usefulness instead of rejecting supervision. Label Revision and Data Selection (LR-DS) revises teacher labels with ground truth and selects a distillation subset by influence; examples outside it keep label supervision. We did not check the journal loss line by line, so we make no claim about its exact CE term \citep{lan2025labelrevision}. RLD refines teacher logits with labels, keeping useful class relations when the teacher's top class is wrong, unlike CTSL's switch; it does not show that every retained relation helps, and its evidence covers CIFAR-100 and ImageNet only \citep{sun2025rld}. CaRE-KD switches between forward and reverse KL by teacher--student confidence and rejects whole batches when the teacher is more uncertain than the student \citep{sengupta2026casd}. CHAD learns sample weights from the alignment between distillation and validation-loss gradients; on BanSum, teacher-confidence and surface-agreement gates stayed within 0.0001 ROUGE-L of cross-entropy, while CHAD improved over standard KD by 0.0173 ROUGE-L, and the paper's multilingual result is for EWAD+CPDP, not CHAD \citep{sumit2026chad}. % VERIFIED: CHAD is a 2026 arXiv preprint; BanSum table reports the cited deltas.

Gradient alignment is a local proxy rather than a causal test, and the gains are specific to low-resource summarisation.

A separate line retrains the teacher instead of filtering it. A teacher trained with MSE to one-hot labels approximates the Bayes conditional probability \citep{hamidi2024mseteacher}; SoTeacher adds Lipschitz and consistency regularisation \citep{dong2024soteacher}; MCMI maximises log-likelihood plus conditional mutual information, and DJIP raises it with differentiable JPEG perturbation \citep{ye2024mcmi,chen2026djip}; and Morad et al.\ show that Bayes-probability teachers reduce student variance under SGD, building them with variational inference and Laplace approximations \citep{morad2026bayesteacher}. Student gains can come with weaker teachers: the MCMI ResNet-34 teacher reaches 71.62 on ImageNet, yet its DKD student reaches 72.06 against 71.54 for KD. DJIP reports 76.61 for WTTM on ResNet32$\times$4$\to$ResNet8$\times$4 and 72.08 for DKD on ImageNet, and the MSE teacher 72.16 on ImageNet under a 120-epoch schedule. These methods need permission to retrain the teacher. % src: ye2024mcmi T2; chen2026djip T1, T3; hamidi2024mseteacher T3; morad2026bayesteacher uses its own protocol (5 seeds) and reports no standard-pair number

At the class or sample level on standard benchmarks, TLLM drops undistillable classes (KD to 75.53, SFTN to 77.83 on ResNet32$\times$4$\to$ResNet8$\times$4) \citep{zhu2022tllm}; BTKD and its IJCV extension BTKD++ separate correct from biased teacher knowledge (both 73.38 on ImageNet) \citep{zhang2025btkd,zhang2026btkdpp}; IPWD uses inverse-propensity weights (76.03 and 71.88 on the two standard pairs) \citep{niu2022ipwd}; and REDistill (a 2026 preprint) down-weights unreliable samples through a power divergence (72.00 on ImageNet) \citep{tybl2026redistill}. KD-AIF weights samples with influence functions but also updates the teacher (to 86.56), so its 82.20 is not comparable with fixed-teacher results \citep{wu2025kdaif}. % src: zhu2022tllm T2; zhang2025btkd T2; zhang2026btkdpp T3; niu2022ipwd T2, T4; wu2025kdaif T2/T3; tybl2026redistill T III

\paragraph{Comparing reliability signals.} Label agreement asks whether the top prediction matches a target and calibration whether confidence matches accuracy; neither says whether a given update helps a given student. CCKD and EA-KD weight, CTSL and Hard Gate KD route, SCKD gates by KD--task gradient similarity, RLD repairs, LR-DS selects, and CHAD learns weights from validation gradients. Their failure cases differ: a label gate inherits annotation errors, a confidence weight trusts confident errors, and a gradient proxy depends on the validation objective. Group methods such as LTKD and AGRE-KD should be judged with group metrics, not ranked beside sample gates on overall accuracy \citep{meng2019conditionalts,mishra2021cckd,lee2022hardgatekd,su2025eakd,sun2025rld,lan2025labelrevision,sumit2026chad,zhu2021sckd,kim2026ltkd,kenfack2025agrekd}.
% INTERPRETATION (restored as prose after review N1)
As a heuristic, not a ranking: selection fits when harmful targets can be replaced by reliable labels, repair when useful class relations survive a wrong top prediction, group-aware methods fit systematic bias, and task-preserving projection fits interference with the student's own objective. The papers span speech, generation, classification, group-robust benchmarks and summarisation, so there is no common protocol \citep{yuan2026tpkd}.
