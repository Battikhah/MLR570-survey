\section{Benchmarks and Reported Results}
\label{sec:benchmarks}

This section collects each included paper's own reported results, not a controlled comparison. The 112 included records and their extracted results are in supplementary Table~S2; the tables here keep the top results and the baselines discussed in the text.

\subsection{Protocol and comparability}
\label{sec:bench-protocol}

Almost every included paper uses the CIFAR-100 protocol of CRD \citep{tian2020crd}, as packaged in
DKD's \texttt{mdistiller} code base \citep{zhao2022dkd}: CRD's pre-trained teachers, 240 epochs, SGD, and
top-1 accuracy on the 10k validation images (\S\ref{sec:prelim-benchmarks}). We organise results around the most reported
pair, ResNet32$\times$4$\to$ResNet8$\times$4, where classical KD reaches 73.33\% \citep[Table~6]{zhao2022dkd}.
WRN-40-2$\to$WRN-16-2 is the second homogeneous pair and the only one most data-free papers report. On
ImageNet-1K the standard pairs are ResNet34$\to$ResNet18 and ResNet50$\to$MobileNetV1.

Three things limit cross-paper comparison even under this protocol. First, papers report different
statistics: the mean of three or five runs, the best run, or a single run.
Differences of about 0.3 points can be smaller than the variation across seeds, but this is an illustrative caution, not a universal significance threshold. Between-paper protocol and reproduction differences are separate sources of uncertainty. Second, many top numbers are \emph{stacked}: a plug-in is added to an already strong method such
as MLKD \citep{jin2023mlkd}, SimKD \citep{chen2022simkd} or CRLD, the base of DeepKD's best row \citep[Table~1]{huang2025deepkd}, so the row measures the combination. The
Variant/configuration column names the base and, where given, the stand-alone number. Third, some rows change the setting:
a retrained or updated teacher (KD-AIF, \citealp{wu2025kdaif}), extra data or augmentation (SRD,
\citealp{miles2024projector}), label smoothing on the student (LSKD and EA-KD rows), online or
multi-student training, a searched distiller, or a stronger KD baseline than \texttt{mdistiller}'s, as in SKD
\citep{jeong2026skd} (KD 74.75). Following the search rulings,
these rows are flagged rather than dropped.

\subsection{CIFAR-100 and ImageNet-1K}
\label{sec:bench-cifar}
\label{sec:bench-imagenet}

Seventy-three records contain a CIFAR-100 result for ResNet32$\times$4$\to$ResNet8$\times$4 (Table~\ref{tab:bench-cifar}).
KD-AIF's 82.20\% is listed but not ranked, because its teacher is updated to 86.56\%. The five highest
configurations among the rest are DeepKD at 79.25\% \citep{huang2025deepkd}, VRM at 78.76\%
\citep{zhang2025vrm}, EA-KD at 78.38\% \citep{su2025eakd}, SKD at 78.33\% \citep{jeong2026skd} and LSKD at
78.28\% \citep{sun2024lskd}. Only VRM and SKD are single methods here: DeepKD is stacked on CRLD, EA-KD's best
row is EA-MLD with label smoothing (EA-KD alone gives 75.46\%), and LSKD's is MLKD with logit
standardisation. Among single methods with an official repository, VRM leads at 78.76\%, followed by SimKD at 78.08\%
\citep{chen2022simkd}, GDKD at 77.76\% \citep{zheng2025gdkd} and LumiNet at 77.50\% \citep{hossain2025luminet}.
The sparse WRN-40-2$\to$WRN-16-2 column has DistPro at 76.36\% \citep{deng2022distpro} and DeepKD at 78.42\%.

\subsection{Data-free and untabulated methods}
\label{sec:bench-datafree}

Ten data-free records (\S\ref{sec:schemes}) are included; nine have WRN-40-2$\to$WRN-16-2 values under their own synthesis budgets, and HiDFD reports an alternative ResNet32$\times$4-to-ShuffleNet pair in the notes. HiDFD and VkD still have no value extracted in the four result columns; their notes identify alternative architecture pairs. SoTeacher and Bayesian-teacher results use their own teachers, and ABKD's extracted values name the stacked ABTTM variant. All are retained in supplementary Table~S2.

\subsection{Comparison across families}
\label{sec:bench-families}

\paragraph{Logit methods.} The most numerous and cheapest group: most single logit methods fall between 76.0\% and 77.8\% (GDKD), the stacked rows above 78\% mostly combine two of them, and on ImageNet they sit within about one point of DKD.

\paragraph{Feature and relation methods.} VRM and SimKD give the highest single-method CIFAR numbers, but
SimKD reuses the teacher's classifier, so the deployed student is larger than ResNet8$\times$4 alone
\citep{chen2022simkd}. Both have author-linked repositories as of 2026-11-14. Feature methods need a projector per pair and intermediate-layer access; several recent ones report only on ImageNet.

\paragraph{Training schemes and reliability.} Online and multi-student schemes
score lower than offline methods but avoid a fixed pre-trained teacher; searched distillers reach 76.6--77.9\% at the price of a search per pair. Reliability results vary by method: RLD reports 76.64\%, MCD 76.81\%, while the updated-teacher KD-AIF row reaches 82.20\% and is not comparable to fixed-teacher rows.

\paragraph{Optimizer-side methods.} DOT (KD+DOT 75.12\%, \citealp{zhao2023dot}), DeepKD (79.25\% stacked on CRLD, \citealp{huang2025deepkd}), DTO-KD (76.40\%, \citealp{hayder2026dtokd}) and DistPro \citep{deng2022distpro} change how the task and distillation gradients are
combined (\S\ref{sec:kd_moo}); none gates the distillation term per sample. PCD \citep{varam2026pcd} has no reported number on these pairs.

\paragraph{A comparison set for combination rules.} Evaluate a method that combines task and distillation objectives against three groups:
\begin{itemize}
\item Combination rules: a tuned weighted sum \citep{hinton2015kd}, MGDA \citep{sener2018mgda}, PCGrad \citep{yu2020pcgrad}, DOT \citep{zhao2023dot} and DTO-KD \citep{hayder2026dtokd}. DeepKD \citep{huang2025deepkd} is quoted from its paper because no code is linked.
\item Loss-side methods: DKD, MLKD, LSKD (MLKD+LS), GDKD and SimKD, where the student retains the teacher classifier.
\item Reliability methods: RLD and MCD.
\end{itemize}
VRM has author-linked code; no author implementation link was located for SKD in the checked sources. Both retain the protocol caveats above.

\paragraph{Applicability.} Without intermediate-layer access, a logit method with linked code
(DKD, MLKD, LSKD, LumiNet) is a practical starting point; for accessible homogeneous pairs, SimKD or a projector-based feature method is stronger on the reported numbers; reliability methods address teacher errors; online schemes avoid a fixed pretrained teacher, while data-free schemes address lack of original training data and still require a teacher.
